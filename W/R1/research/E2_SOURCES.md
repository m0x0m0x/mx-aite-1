### Notes on tier assignment

- **E2-D1** is Tier D rather than C: 1kx is a recognised research house (investment firm with a published research practice), not a measurement provider in the CoinGecko/DeFiLlama sense. Its protocol-level data is secondary (aggregated from Dune, Token Terminal, DeFiLlama; valuations from CoinGecko), but its *protocol census* (1,244 protocols) is its own contribution and is the number the single-gate recommendation rests on.
- **E2-A1 / E2-D2** are the same document; A1 is listed separately only because the base-rate figure is cited early in E2.1.3. A1's Tier D is intentional — it is a preprint.
- **E2-A2** is the only Tier A source in the slice, and it is used only for base-rate context (US complaint and loss volumes), never for project-level claims.
