Tier key: **A** regulator / government / court record · **B** primary technical (official post-mortem, verified contract or on-chain trace, filed expert report) · **C** primary measurement (DeFiLlama, L2BEAT, CertiK, Chainalysis, RWA.xyz, Dune, CoinGecko) · **D** established long-form investigation by a named journalist at a recognised outlet, or academic research · **E** BANNED as citation ground (blogs, listing sites, SEO, airdrop listicles) — used only to locate an A–D source, which is then fetched and cited.

Notes on tiering choices that needed a judgement call:
- Circle's monthly attestation PDFs are issuer-published but examined by a Big Four firm under AICPA standards → **B** (primary document), not A, because the issuer selects the scope. A 2024 examination report is cited separately to show the same document existed around a depeg episode.
- CertiK / Chainalysis incident analyses are measurement providers → **C**.
- Press write-ups of a numeric incident where I could not reach a primary or measurement source are **D** and are flagged inline as *not primary*.
- Three figures are explicitly left unsourced in the case file and are listed at the bottom.
