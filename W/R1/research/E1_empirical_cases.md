# E1 — Empirical Case Studies

Scope: **named case evidence only.** Each row states what actually happened, the metric that separates it from speculation, and — for failures — the signal that was *observable before the loss*. Scoring signals derived from these cases are at the end; ref IDs resolve in `E1_SOURCES.md`.

A note on how numbers are handled: figures below are quoted from DeFiLlama / L2BEAT / CertiK / a court's filing / an on-chain trace report as of the access date (2026-10-06), except where the cell is explicitly marked. Anything I could not tie to such a source is written `UNSOURCED` rather than estimated.

## E1.3 Cases the tool must not get wrong

| case | why the surface signals mislead | what the tool should and should not conclude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MakerDAO / Sky after March 2020 ("Black Thursday")** | A flagship, audited, 2-year-old protocol left **$4.5M of unbacked DAI** and over **$8M of ETH was sold for free** in zero-bid auctions during a 43% one-day ETH drop (E1-D17). A naive "protocol insolvency" flag fires. | **Should:** conclude that *liquidation machinery failed under oracle/gas stress*, not that the protocol was a fraud — and that the loss was **socialised through MKR dilution via debt auction**, a public, token-funded recovery. **Should not:** conclude "fraud" or "insolvent"; the same protocol is now a top-3 revenue protocol (E1-C18, E1-D17). A flag on "unbacked debt" is correct only if paired with *who bore the loss and was it disclosed*. |
| **USDC / Circle** | Its product is indistinguishable from an algorithmic or partially-collateralised stablecoin from on-chain data alone — the reserve is entirely off-chain — and USDC **has** broken peg materially: **$0.88 on 11 Mar 2023** after SVB exposure was revealed (E1-D18, Reuters), and repeated flash prints to **$0.74** on Binance on 3 Jan 2024 that recovered within minutes (E1-D20).
